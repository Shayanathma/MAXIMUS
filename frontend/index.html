Why This Software Matters

The integration of traditional AYUSH medicine with modern healthcare systems requires standardized terminology mapping. Our NAMASTE to ICD-11 service enables:

Interoperability: Seamless integration between traditional AYUSH Electronic Medical Records (EMRs) and modern healthcare systems using FHIR-compliant terminology mapping.
Insurance Processing: Enables insurance claims processing for AYUSH treatments by providing internationally recognized ICD-11 codes alongside traditional NAMASTE classifications.
Research & Analytics: Facilitates medical research and health analytics by bridging traditional medicine terminologies with standardized international classifications.
Global Healthcare: Supports India's healthcare digitization goals by making traditional medicine data compatible with global health information systems.
